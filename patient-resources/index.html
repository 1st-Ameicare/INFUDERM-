01 What services does INFUDERM Clinic offer? +

INFUDERM Clinic offers personalized medical aesthetics and wellness treatments, including Botox®, dermal fillers, facial balancing, laser hair removal, microneedling, Morpheus8®, PRP/PRF, IV therapy, medical weight loss, facials, skin rejuvenation, and other advanced aesthetic services.

INFUDERM Clinic has two convenient locations in Northern Virginia:

📍 Reston, VA

📍 Aldie, VA

We proudly serve patients throughout Loudoun County, Fairfax County, and nearby communities.

03 Which areas do you serve? +

Patients visit INFUDERM Clinic from Reston, Aldie, Ashburn, Herndon, Sterling, Chantilly, South Riding, Brambleton, Stone Ridge, Leesburg, Fairfax, Vienna, Great Falls, McLean, Tysons, and surrounding Northern Virginia communities.

04 Why choose INFUDERM Clinic? +

INFUDERM Clinic focuses on personalized treatment plans, natural-looking results, advanced technology, and patient-centered care. Every consultation is tailored to your unique goals, facial anatomy, skin concerns, and overall wellness.

05 Do you offer FREE consultations? +

Yes. We offer complimentary consultations for many of our aesthetic and wellness treatments. During your consultation, our experienced providers will discuss your goals, answer your questions, and recommend a personalized treatment plan.

06 Do I need a consultation before treatment? +

Yes. Most treatments begin with a consultation to evaluate your concerns, medical history, and desired results. This helps ensure your treatment plan is both safe and personalized.

07 Are treatments personalized? +

Absolutely. No two patients are alike. Every treatment plan is customized based on your goals, facial structure, skin condition, and provider recommendations.

08 Do you treat both women and men? +

Yes. INFUDERM Clinic welcomes both women and men seeking aesthetic treatments, skin rejuvenation, wellness services, and medical weight loss.

09 What aesthetic treatments are most popular? +

At INFUDERM Clinic, we believe aesthetic medicine should enhance your natural beauty—not change who you are. Our personalized approach, evidence-based treatments, and commitment to patient education help every patient make informed decisions with confidence.

19 What services does a medical spa provide? +

Medical spas combine aesthetic treatments with medically supervised care. Common services include Botox®, dermal fillers, laser hair removal, skin rejuvenation, microneedling, IV therapy, and medical weight loss.

20 Can I combine multiple treatments during one visit? +

Many treatments can be combined safely depending on your goals and provider recommendations. During your consultation, we'll discuss which combination treatments are appropriate for you.

Yes, many patients can complete both their consultation and treatment during the same visit, depending on the treatment, provider availability, and medical assessment. Please let us know when booking if you would like to be considered for a same-day treatment.
