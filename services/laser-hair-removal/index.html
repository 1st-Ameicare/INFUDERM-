When It May Not Work Well

Important Hair & Skin Considerations

Laser hair removal works best on hair that contains pigment. Very light blonde, gray, white, or red hair may respond less predictably because the laser needs pigment to target the follicle.

Your provider will evaluate your hair color, hair thickness, skin tone, and treatment area before recommending treatment.

Sessions

How Many Laser Hair Removal Sessions Will I Need?

Most patients need a series of laser hair removal sessions because hair grows in cycles and not every follicle is active at the same time.

The number of sessions may vary based on treatment area, hair color, hair thickness, skin type, hormones, and individual response to treatment.

Maintenance treatments may be recommended for long-term results.

Results

When Will I See Results?

Many patients begin noticing reduced hair growth after several sessions. Results improve progressively throughout the treatment series.

Because each person’s hair growth cycle is different, your provider will review realistic expectations during your consultation.

Before Treatment

Laser Hair Removal Pre-Care

  • Avoid excessive sun exposure before treatment
  • Do not wax, tweeze, or pluck the treatment area before sessions
  • Shave the area as instructed before your appointment
  • Avoid harsh exfoliants or irritating products as advised
  • Share medication, medical history, and skin concerns with your provider
After Treatment

Laser Hair Removal Aftercare

  • Wear sunscreen on exposed treated areas
  • Avoid excessive heat, saunas, and hot tubs temporarily
  • Avoid harsh products if skin feels sensitive
  • Do not pick or irritate the treated area
  • Follow all provider aftercare instructions
Safety

Is Laser Hair Removal Safe?

Laser hair removal can be safe and effective when performed by trained professionals using appropriate settings and safety protocols.

At INFUDERM, your provider evaluates your skin type, hair type, medical history, and treatment area before recommending laser hair removal.

Temporary redness, warmth, mild swelling, or sensitivity may occur after treatment.

Who Should Avoid Treatment?

When Laser Hair Removal May Need to Wait

Treatment may need to be delayed or modified for individuals with:

  • Recent sunburn or excessive tanning
  • Active skin infection in the treatment area
  • Open wounds or irritation
  • Certain photosensitizing medications
  • Certain medical conditions
  • Pregnancy or nursing, depending on provider guidance

A consultation is required to confirm candidacy.
