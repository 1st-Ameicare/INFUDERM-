What Is HydraFacial?

A Complete Skin Refresh in One Treatment

HydraFacial is a non-invasive skin treatment that uses a specialized device to cleanse, exfoliate, extract impurities, and infuse the skin with hydrating and nourishing ingredients.

Unlike a traditional facial, HydraFacial uses a consistent, technology-driven process designed to support smoother, clearer, more radiant-looking skin.

How It Works

The HydraFacial Process

HydraFacial is performed in a step-by-step sequence to refresh and hydrate the skin.

1

Cleanse + Exfoliate

Dead skin cells and surface impurities are gently removed.

2

Gentle Peel

A light resurfacing step helps loosen buildup without harsh downtime.

3

Extraction

Vortex suction helps remove congestion and impurities from pores.

4

Hydration

Hydrating ingredients are infused into the skin.

5

Protection

Finishing skincare and SPF guidance help protect the skin after treatment.

Before Treatment

HydraFacial Pre-Care

  • Avoid excessive sun exposure
  • Avoid harsh exfoliants before treatment
  • Pause retinoids or active products as instructed
  • Arrive with clean skin when possible
  • Tell your provider about sensitivities or recent procedures
After Treatment

HydraFacial Aftercare

  • Avoid excessive heat for the first 24 hours
  • Avoid harsh exfoliants immediately after treatment
  • Keep skin hydrated
  • Wear sunscreen daily
  • Follow all provider recommendations
