What Is Botox®?

Botox® Cosmetic is an FDA-approved prescription injectable treatment used to temporarily reduce the appearance of dynamic facial wrinkles caused by repeated muscle movement.

Dynamic wrinkles develop over time from everyday facial expressions such as smiling, frowning, squinting, and raising your eyebrows.

Botox® works by temporarily relaxing targeted facial muscles responsible for creating these expression lines. As muscle activity is reduced, the overlying skin appears smoother, helping soften existing wrinkles while reducing the formation of deeper lines over time.

Botox® has become one of the world's most popular non-surgical cosmetic treatments because it offers predictable, natural-looking results with little to no downtime when administered by qualified medical professionals.

At INFUDERM Clinic, Botox® treatments are never one-size-fits-all. Every consultation begins with a thorough facial assessment so we can understand your concerns, facial anatomy, muscle movement, and desired outcome before recommending a personalized treatment plan.

How Does Botox® Work?

Facial muscles naturally contract whenever you smile, laugh, frown, or raise your eyebrows. Over time, these repeated movements cause the skin above those muscles to fold repeatedly, eventually forming visible expression lines.

Botox® temporarily blocks communication between specific nerves and targeted muscles. As those muscles relax, they contract less forcefully, allowing the skin to appear smoother.

Rather than changing your facial structure, Botox® simply reduces excessive muscle movement in carefully selected areas.

This allows patients to enjoy a refreshed appearance while maintaining natural facial movement.

Common Botox® Treatment Areas

Botox® can be used to address multiple areas of the face depending on your concerns and treatment goals.

Forehead Lines

Horizontal forehead lines are among the most common concerns patients experience as they age. Botox® helps soften these expression lines while maintaining natural eyebrow movement.

Frown Lines (Glabellar Lines)

Often called the "11 lines," these vertical lines between the eyebrows can create an appearance of stress or fatigue. Botox® relaxes the muscles responsible for these lines, creating a smoother, more approachable appearance.

Crow's Feet

The fine lines that develop around the outer corners of the eyes become more noticeable when smiling. Botox® helps soften crow's feet while preserving a natural smile.

Bunny Lines

Small wrinkles that appear along the bridge of the nose may also be treated with Botox® when appropriate.

Brow Lift

Strategic Botox® placement may help create a subtle lifting effect for the eyebrows, resulting in a more refreshed appearance.

Lip Flip

A Botox® Lip Flip relaxes specific muscles around the upper lip, allowing the lip to appear slightly fuller without adding volume through fillers.

Chin Dimpling

Botox® can help relax muscles responsible for an uneven or dimpled chin appearance, creating smoother contours.

Downturned Corners of the Mouth

Certain facial muscles naturally pull the corners of the mouth downward over time. Botox® may help relax these muscles, creating a more balanced appearance.

Neck Bands (Platysmal Bands)

Botox® may be used to soften prominent vertical neck bands for appropriate candidates during a comprehensive facial assessment.

Masseter (Jawline)

For some patients, Botox® may be recommended to relax enlarged jaw muscles associated with teeth grinding or to create a slimmer jawline when clinically appropriate.
