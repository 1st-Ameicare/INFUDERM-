What Are Dermal Fillers?

Dermal fillers are injectable treatments commonly used to restore lost facial volume, enhance facial contours, improve facial balance, and soften the appearance of certain lines and folds.

Many fillers are made with hyaluronic acid, a substance naturally found in the body that helps the skin retain moisture and support.

As we age, our skin gradually loses collagen, elastin, and facial volume. This natural process can contribute to thinner lips, flatter cheeks, deeper smile lines, and reduced definition in areas such as the chin and jawline. Dermal fillers help restore structure and support where volume has diminished, creating refreshed, natural-looking results.

At INFUDERM Clinic, every filler treatment is personalized. We carefully evaluate your facial anatomy, proportions, and aesthetic goals before recommending a treatment plan designed to complement your natural features rather than change them.

Our philosophy is simple:

Enhance your natural beauty—not someone else's.

What Is Facial Balancing?

Facial balancing is a personalized approach to aesthetic treatment that focuses on improving the harmony and proportion between different facial features.

Rather than concentrating on one concern alone, facial balancing considers how the lips, cheeks, chin, jawline, and other facial structures work together to create a naturally balanced appearance.

For example, enhancing chin projection may improve facial profile, while restoring cheek volume can support the mid-face and soften surrounding lines. In some cases, subtle improvements in multiple areas create more natural results than treating a single feature alone.
