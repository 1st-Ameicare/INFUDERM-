Understanding GLP-1 Weight Loss Therapy

What Are GLP-1 Injections?

GLP-1 (Glucagon-Like Peptide-1) medications are prescription treatments that help regulate appetite and support weight management.

These medications work by mimicking naturally occurring hormones involved in hunger and digestion, helping patients feel fuller for longer and better manage food intake.

Treatment options may include Semaglutide and Tirzepatide, depending on your provider’s evaluation and recommendations.

Common Side Effects

What Should I Expect?

Like any prescription medication, GLP-1 treatments may cause side effects in some individuals.

Common side effects may include:

  • Nausea
  • Bloating
  • Constipation
  • Diarrhea
  • Stomach discomfort
  • Reduced appetite

These effects are often temporary and may improve as your body adjusts to treatment. Your provider will review potential risks, benefits, and safety considerations before starting therapy.
