What Is Microneedling?

Collagen-Stimulating Skin Rejuvenation

Microneedling creates controlled micro-injuries in the skin using fine sterile needles. These micro-channels trigger the body’s natural healing response and stimulate collagen and elastin production.

Over time, this collagen-building process can help improve texture, smoothness, acne scarring, fine lines, and overall skin quality.

What Is SkinPen®?

FDA-Cleared Microneedling Technology

SkinPen® is an FDA-cleared Microneedling device designed for precise, controlled treatment.

It allows the provider to customize treatment depth based on your skin concerns and treatment area, supporting safety and effective results.

Not Always Recommended

When Microneedling May Need to Wait

Microneedling may not be appropriate for everyone at every time. Additional evaluation may be needed if you have:

  • Active skin infection
  • Active acne flare in the treatment area
  • Recent sunburn
  • Open wounds or irritation
  • Certain medical conditions affecting healing
  • Recent use of certain medications or treatments

Your provider will review your medical history and skin condition before treatment.

Treatment Options

Microneedling With PRP or PRF

Microneedling may be combined with PRP or PRF to support enhanced skin rejuvenation. PRP and PRF use components derived from your own blood to support healing and collagen stimulation.

Your provider will recommend whether standard Microneedling or Microneedling with PRP/PRF is most appropriate.

Comparison

Microneedling vs RF Microneedling

Traditional Microneedling uses controlled micro-channels to stimulate collagen. RF Microneedling also delivers radiofrequency energy into deeper layers of the skin.

Traditional Microneedling may be recommended for texture, pores, and acne scars. RF Microneedling may be preferred when skin tightening is also a goal.

Results

How Many Sessions Will I Need?

The number of treatments varies based on your skin concerns, goals, and response to treatment.

Many patients benefit from a series of sessions spaced several weeks apart. Maintenance treatments may be recommended as part of a long-term skin health plan.

Timeline

When Will I See Results?

Some patients notice early improvement in skin texture and radiance. Collagen remodeling takes time, so results often continue to improve over several weeks to months.

Your provider will discuss realistic expectations during your consultation.
