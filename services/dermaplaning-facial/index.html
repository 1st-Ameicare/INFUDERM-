What Is a Dermaplaning Facial?

Surface-Level Exfoliation for a Fresh Glow

Dermaplaning uses a sterile surgical blade to gently remove dead skin cells and vellus hair, often called peach fuzz, from the skin surface.

This controlled exfoliation may help reveal fresher, smoother-looking skin and improve how skincare products and makeup apply.

How It Works

Gentle, Precise, Non-Invasive

During treatment, your provider uses a sterile blade at a precise angle to gently exfoliate the outermost layer of the skin.

The treatment is designed to be comfortable and does not involve chemicals, needles, or significant downtime.

When to Wait

When Dermaplaning May Not Be Appropriate

Dermaplaning may need to be delayed or avoided if you have:

  • Active acne flare
  • Open wounds or cuts
  • Active skin infection
  • Recent sunburn
  • Significant skin irritation
  • Certain recent procedures or medications

Your provider will review your skin before treatment.

After Treatment
